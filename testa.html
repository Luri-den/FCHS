Before You Explore!

This map is a testbed for Discover Historic Connersville. Information is being assembled from ongoing research and may be incomplete.

Access and Private Property

A location's appearance on this map does not give anyone the right to enter it. Always obtain permission before entering private property and respect posted signs.

Protecting Historic Places

Please do not disturb, move, remove, or damage historic objects, structures, markers, or other features. Treat historic places with care.

About This Information

This is a work in progress. Records may be incomplete, approximate, or contain errors. Locations are for general reference and should not be used for legal, boundary, surveying, or property decisions.

Before You Use Location

This feature shows your approximate position on the map for general reference only.

  • It is not turn-by-turn navigation.
  • It is not route planning or travel directions.
  • It is not surveying or legal boundary determination.

GPS accuracy varies by device and conditions. Map locations are approximate. In an emergency, call 911.
